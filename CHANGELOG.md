### Features

- Add the `market_radar` package skeleton (src layout, `market-radar` CLI entry point, pinned `requirements.txt`, ruff and pytest config), typed settings loaded from `config/` (`settings.yaml`, `universe.yaml` with VN30, banks and 8 sectors, `holidays.yaml`, `portfolio.example.yaml`) and a trading calendar that projects future sessions ([`1110fbf`](https://github.com/nguyenbaanh7779/market-radar/commit/1110fbf9dfa80af0dc962c2d12bb927d6b407dbe))
- Add the data layer: daily bars from the keyless Arena (ckai.vn) API by default or vnstock v6 with a broker key, one Parquet file per symbol with incremental updates, DuckDB loading, and quality checks (gaps, inconsistent OHLC, moves beyond the exchange price limit, cross-source differences) ([`61a0a59`](https://github.com/nguyenbaanh7779/market-radar/commit/61a0a59e11f8501d48142be5e3920e8eceb1b625))
- Add Module 1 signals: pandas-only indicators, market state (VNINDEX trend, volatility percentile, breadth, liquidity), equal-weight sector baskets with an RRG-style rotation view, strong/caution flags per stock and the status of the user's holdings ([`37f3e66`](https://github.com/nguyenbaanh7779/market-radar/commit/37f3e669c01f6315826ecb1e449e562fb3f612f8))
- Add Module 2, the direction forecaster: UP/DOWN/SIDEWAY labels over 1 and 5 sessions with Arena's ±0.2% band (voided across suspect jumps), point-in-time features, `majority`, `momentum`, `logistic` and `gbdt` (LightGBM with TreeSHAP drivers) behind one `ForecastAgent` interface, purged and embargoed walk-forward validation, and a Diebold–Mariano test on daily log loss ([`92ae5c8`](https://github.com/nguyenbaanh7779/market-radar/commit/92ae5c84aba906e3da749eb752fab10da80fa35f))
- Add Module 3, paper trading: a cash account with 100-share lots, T+2, 0.15% fees and 0.1% selling tax; top-5 stocks by P(UP) over 5 sessions weighted by P(UP) − P(DOWN), rebalanced every 5 sessions with a daily 7% stop-loss, filled at the next close; a backtest against buy-and-hold of VN30 ([`145bc90`](https://github.com/nguyenbaanh7779/market-radar/commit/145bc907c2de5779ec8bb3ee690ca8216fef68d9), [`b88219e`](https://github.com/nguyenbaanh7779/market-radar/commit/b88219ef9bf846f51823738a9d375d0ecb67245d))
- Add the daily pipeline and CLI (`update`, `check`, `signals`, `evaluate`, `backtest`, `daily`): forecasts of every model logged before their outcome, resolution of past forecasts, a forward paper account, and a newsletter sent to Telegram, with idempotent CSV logs and the personal portfolio kept out of saved copies ([`0fc51e4`](https://github.com/nguyenbaanh7779/market-radar/commit/0fc51e43ac14d535a07f4e3b9674eb22fc6d229d))
